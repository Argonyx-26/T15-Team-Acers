## How to use the packages

For each crop problem, capture the exact package title, edition/year, page
number, URL or PDF, and date accessed. Then compare the package with the
current product label. The app-ready record should not be marked complete until
both agree on:

- crop and target organism;
- active ingredient, concentration, and formulation;
- dose, water volume, method, and timing;
- pre-harvest or tapping interval;
- PPE, re-entry, livestock, pollinator, and water-body restrictions.

Some institutional sites are difficult to index or may move PDFs. In that case,
download the document from the institution's official site and store its title,
edition, page number, and access date in the source register.
